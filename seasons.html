When to Visit

Seasons & Water Flow

The experience changes dramatically throughout the year based on water volume, rainfall patterns, and safari seasons.

Feb — May

High Water Season

The Zambezi is swollen with rain. Power and spray are at maximum capacity. Expect thunderous noise, heavy mist, and drenched forest walkways.

Jun — Aug

Mid Water / Peak Safari

Clear skies, moderate water levels, and excellent visibility. Excellent balance for both gorge viewing, photography, and game safaris.

Sep — Jan

Low Water Season

Water levels drop revealing the rock face. Ideal for white-water rafting, visiting Devil's Pool, and viewing cliff structures clearly.

FAQ

Common Questions About Seasons

Find the best time to go, what to expect in each season, and how to plan around weather and water levels.

When is the best time to see the falls at full flow?

The best high-water experience is between February and May, when the Zambezi is at its peak flow and the falls are thunderous, misty, and dramatic.

Which season is best for game drives?

June to August is ideal for game viewing, as water levels drop, wildlife gathers around rivers, and the skies are usually clear and dry.

Can I visit Devil’s Pool year-round?

Devil’s Pool is usually accessible from late August through early January when water levels are lower and the pool forms safely on the Zambian side.

What should I pack for each season?

Bring rainproof gear and quick-dry layers for the wet months, sun protection for the drier season, and a light jacket for cool early mornings during winter.
