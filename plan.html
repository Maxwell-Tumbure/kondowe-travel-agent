Essential Information

Travel Logistics & Tips

Visas, entry points, currencies, and essential advice for visiting Zimbabwe and Zambia.

Border Crossing

KAZA UniVisa

Allows unlimited travel between Zimbabwe and Zambia for up to 30 days, including day trips to Botswana via Kazungula.

Currency

US Dollars & Cards

US Dollars are widely accepted in both Victoria Falls (Zimbabwe) and Livingstone (Zambia). Visa and Mastercard are used in major hotels.

Health & Safety

Malaria Precautions

Victoria Falls is a low-to-moderate risk malaria area. Mosquito repellent and prophylaxis are strongly recommended during rainy months.
